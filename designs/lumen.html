    About

    Lumen is the working name of Noor Hallin, a landscape photographer and director based between Bergen and Lisbon.

    She works slowly: medium-format film, long waits, the same valley revisited until it gives something back. The films are short and mostly wordless; the prints are large and quiet. Commissions are welcome where the weather gets the final say.

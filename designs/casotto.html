(01) The studio

Casotto is a six-person studio that makes a racket on purpose. We name things, draw things and build things: identities, packaging and websites for coffee roasters, swim clubs, gazzosa makers and one very famous lavanderia. Designers and developers, one table, no hand-offs. Loud results.

(04) Process

How we work

Four steps, no mystery. Most projects take ten to fourteen weeks from the first hello to launch day.

  1. Dig in 01
  2. Find the idea 02
  3. Make it 03
  4. Ship it together 04
01020304/ 04
Step oneWeeks 1–2

Dig in

We talk to your team, your regulars and whoever answers the phone. Then we tell you, plainly, what we heard.

You getA workshop, a brand audit and a one-page brief everyone signs.

01
Step twoWeeks 3–5

Find the idea

One sentence the whole brand can hang from. If it doesn’t make the room grin, it isn’t the one yet.

You getPositioning, naming if you need it, and the big idea on a single page.

02
Step threeWeeks 6–11

Make it

Logo, type, colour, voice, packaging and website, designed together in one room so nothing feels bolted on.

You getThe identity system, packaging artwork and a site built by us.

03
Step fourWeek 12 onwards

Ship it together

The people who drew it and the people who coded it press the button side by side, then read the first week’s numbers with you.

You getA launch kit, a component library and guidelines that update with the site.

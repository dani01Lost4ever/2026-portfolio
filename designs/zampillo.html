Why Zampillo

We grew up on bitter chinotto from the kiosk on the Lido and blood oranges in January. Zampillo is that feeling in a can: fruit from small Italian growers, garden herbs and a gentle fizz, with half the sugar of a regular soda. Nothing syrupy, nothing fake, nothing you’d need to look up.

  • 38kcal in a can of Arancia Rossa
  • 14%pressed Italian fruit, not concentrate
  • 0sweeteners, colourings or syrups
  • 100%recyclable aluminium, again and again

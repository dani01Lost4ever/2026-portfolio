Project 07 — Casa Abierta 2026Bogotá, ColombiaRoom 12 · 42 m²

The RiverRoom

A living room built for one slow afternoon: lime plaster, wood from old river barges and fique thread spun with a single strand of brass.

Scroll

About the room

(01) The brief

We were given a white box with no window and eleven weeks. We gave it a riverbank at five in the afternoon: everything low, warm and a little worn, so that people would sit down before they knew why.

(03) Materials

Five materials,none of them new

01 / 05Scroll to walk the wall
01

Woven iraca palm

Usiacurí, Atlántico · hand-woven

Split palm leaf, boiled and sun-dried, woven in a double basket weave for the pendant and the panels behind the sofa.

02

Reclaimed river wood

Honda, Tolima · Magdalena barges

Decking from three cargo barges retired in 2019. We kept the bolt holes and the silt line.

03

Fique with brass

Curití, Santander · spun on site

Natural fique spun with one strand of brass every nine centimetres, so the curtain wall flickers when the light moves.

04

Corrugated metal

Fredonia, Antioquia · coffee-farm roofs

Painted roofing from a drying shed, flattened only where it had to be. It lines the reveals of every arch.

05

Lime plaster

Nobsa, Boyacá · three coats

Hydrated lime and river sand, burnished with a steel trowel and waxed with beeswax. It breathes; so does the room.

Every piece went back to its maker when the showcase closed.The sofa now sits in a reading room in Honda, a hundred metres from the river its plinth came out of.

(04) Plan

Drawn aroundone long sofa

We started with the sofa and the 4 pm patch of sun, and let every wall answer to those two. Five moments, in the order people found them.

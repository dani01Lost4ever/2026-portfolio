01 — The place

Forty hours of sound inside a decommissioned power station on the lagoon. Three rooms, no clocks, and a night ferry that runs until the sun comes up.

Kaltwerk made electricity for Port Ostra for sixty years. Since 2021 it makes noise for one weekend a year. The turbines are gone; the acoustics stayed.

06 — Getting there

Train, ferry, four hundred metres of pier.

  1. 01

    Ostra Centrale

    Regional trains from the city every 30 minutes until 00:40. Follow the line ten minutes down Via dei Cantieri to Pier 4.

  2. 02

    Night ferry

    Every 20 minutes from 17:00 to 07:00, twelve minutes across the lagoon. Your wristband is your ferry ticket.

  3. 03

    Kaltwerk gate

    Four hundred metres along the old coal pier. Lockers, water and first aid are right inside the gate.

Why “ombra”

The shade of the campanile

In Venice a glass of wine is called an ombra, a shade. The old wine sellers of the piazza chased the shadow of the campanile to keep their bottles cool. We don't chase anything. We pour small glasses, fry sardines at eleven, argue about football at seven, and keep a bench by the water for whoever gets there first.

— Nane & Bepi hosts, pourers, dishwashers

How it works

From scattered data to one number everyone trusts.

  1. 01 — CONNECT

    Plug in your sources.

    Payments, your database, product events and CRM. Tacca backfills two years of history while you get a coffee. No pipelines to babysit.

  2. 02 — BUILD

    Ask a question, get a chart.

    Pick a metric, group it, slice it. Every chart is a saved query you can open, tweak and trust, so nobody argues about whose number is right.

  3. 03 — SHARE

    Send it where people already are.

    Live links, scheduled digests and alerts in your team channels, with the chart attached. Viewers are free on every plan.

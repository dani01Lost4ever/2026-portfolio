Project 07 — Private study, 2026Cannaregio, VeneziaPiano nobile · 32 m²

IlRiflesso

A north-facing study on a canal three metres wide. It never gets the sun, so every surface in it is there to catch what the water throws up instead.

About the room

(01) The brief

The room faced north onto a canal three metres wide and never saw the sun. So we let the water do the lighting: every surface is pale, polished or strung with glass, and the light the canal throws up lands on the ceiling, still moving, long after the boats have gone.

(03) Materials

Five materials,chosen by the water

01 / 05Scroll to walk the wall
01

Marmorino

Lime and marble dust · four coats

Slaked lime and crushed marble, laid thin and burnished with a steel trowel until it shines like skin. It breathes; so does the room.

02

Terrazzo alla veneziana

Poured in place · ground by hand

Marble chips in a bed of lime and marble dust, rolled flat, left a month to cure, then ground and oiled. The floor moves with the house; hairline cracks are allowed.

03

Istrian stone

Sill, table, niche shelf · honed

The dense, pale limestone Venice builds its thresholds from, where water meets wall. Honed rather than polished, so it holds the light instead of flashing it back.

04

Murano glass beads

Portego curtain · strung on linen

Nine thousand beads, mostly clear, in pale and bottle green, with the odd amber-gilt one. When a boat passes, the whole doorway flickers.

05

Undyed linen

Daybed, pendant, blinds · left raw

Heavy linen left the colour of the flax. It softens with every wash and takes the canal light the way paper takes ink.

Every sample was tested in the room at night, under one lamp.Anything that looked dead once the canal went dark was cut. Oak, brass and three whites went that way.

(04) Plan

Drawn aroundone long daybed

We started with the daybed and the patch of water-light on the ceiling above it, and let every wall answer to those two. Five moments, in the order the client meets them each night.
